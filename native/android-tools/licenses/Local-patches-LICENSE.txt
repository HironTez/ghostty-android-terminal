The patches are local changes against pinned upstream releases, not copies
of Termux patches. Android passwd behavior and known-hosts hard-link
limitations were researched with Termux as portability reference:
https://github.com/termux/termux-packages/tree/master/packages/openssh
The known_hosts change (0003-atomic-known-hosts.patch, android-known-hosts.h)
is independent: it backs up by durable copy instead of hard link or rename,
replaces the file with one fsync'd atomic rename, and serializes writers with
flock().
